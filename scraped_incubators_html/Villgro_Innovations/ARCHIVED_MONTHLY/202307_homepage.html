Who are we?

Villgro is one of India’s pioneering incubators of social enterprises. We believe that innovation and market based models are powerful solutions to alleviating poverty and creating social impact at scale. We have spent the last 20 years perfecting our incubation model towards this goal.

Focus Sectors

We have built intelligence and expertise in three main sectors.

Healthcare

Improving health outcomes at the Bottom of the Pyramid through affordable, accessible and innovative solutions.

Agriculture

Building resilience of the farmer as well as the farming supply chain.

Current Programs

We run the following main programs.

TVARAN: Accelerating Women Entrepreneurs driving Climate Resilience

TVARAN is a 10-month-long unique market access program launched by Villgro and an initiative by Cisco Corporate Social Responsibility to accelerate women entrepreneurs driving innovative solutions in Climate Action.

Know More

Sustainable Mobility Challenge

The ‘Sustainable Mobility Challenge’ is a multi-pronged program targeting startups and enterprises at different stages of maturity who are focussed on solving problems related to sustainable mobility. 

Know More

Climate Smart Agriculture

The Climate Smart Agriculture Program aims to support startups working to mitigate the environmental footprint of Indian Agriculture by using technology to reduce the use of inputs, water and emissions from agricultural activities while increasing productivity.

Know More

Yash Entrepreneurs Program

The Program, supported by USAID’s MOMENTUM Country and Global Leadership: India-Yash, envisions creating a strong ecosystem of mid to late-stage investment-ready enterprises, addressing critical challenges in the Sexual and Reproductive Health space.

Startup India Seed Fund Scheme

The Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS) aims to provide financial assistance to startups for proof of concept, prototype development, product trials, market entry, and commercialization.

ShelterTech & Housing Accelerator Program

The ShelterTech program aims to support the incubation and launch of high-impact, highly-scalable, innovation-based sustainable & affordable housing startups registered in India.
