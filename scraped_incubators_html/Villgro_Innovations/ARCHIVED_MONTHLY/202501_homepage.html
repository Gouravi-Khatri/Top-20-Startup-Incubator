Our journey over the years

Villgro was founded in 2001 by Paul Basil, with a mission to create impactful, innovative and successful social enterprises.

Rural Innovations Network was founded in 2001 by Paul Basil, with a mission to create impactful, innovative, and successful social enterprises.

Core to our work is the belief that market-based models are a powerful way to solve social problems and create impact at scale. By capacitating these models with the right resources and knowledge, they are a sustainable way of creating lasting social impact.

Hear from the community

“
For nearly two decades, Villgro has been an invaluable partner to us – supporting early-stage inventions to develop into self-sustaining enterprises, and delivering value to underserved markets throughout India. Villgro has evolved to be more than an incubator; it develops markets, financial solutions and shares insights and learnings that inform our evolving understanding of what systems support innovation and entrepreneurship to have an impact.
Maggie Flanagan Program Officer,
The Lemelson Foundation
Villgro’s team has profound experience in the ecosystem of livelihoods and stands out in the rural DRE sector owing to the following aspects: Reaching beyond technology showcase and focusing on scaling entrepreneurial innovations. This involves active engagement of market players, financing partners and policymakers, which no other programme has managed to do at scale in India.”
DOEN Foundation
